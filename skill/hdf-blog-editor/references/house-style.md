# HDF house style

## Voice

Write warmly, specifically, honestly and without hurry. Show capable service without inflating it. Preserve dignity and avoid using suffering as leverage.

Use concrete detail to support emotion. Put the person or family before HDF when natural. State limitations instead of smoothing them away.

Preserve the strongest human idea, rhythm or memorable framing in the source when it remains appropriate. Improve its judgment and clarity instead of replacing the article with a generic nonprofit template.

## Final refinement check

Before delivering a full rewrite:

- Read it once without considering SEO. It should sound written for a person, not assembled to satisfy a checklist.
- Remove repeated abstractions such as `care`, `trust`, `transparency`, `impact` and `dignity` when concrete language already carries the meaning.
- Combine thin or repetitive sections. Do not create a new heading merely to improve Yoast distribution.
- Remove sentences that only restate the sentence before them.
- Vary paragraph and sentence rhythm naturally without manufacturing fragments or transitions.
- Check that the title is distinctive to the story rather than reusable across many HDF posts.
- Confirm that technology, statistics and HDF itself support the human story rather than displacing it.
- Preserve useful personality from the original, but remove comparisons or humor that trivialize worship, hardship or entrusted giving.
- Use American spelling consistently, including `fulfill` and `fulfillment`.

## Audience

Write first for existing donors, then prospective donors, HDF's community, and search visitors. Do not make a donor-trust story sound like a database entry.

## Language

- Use American spelling in posts: `program`, `center`, `organization`.
- Capitalize **Iftar** every time.
- Write **Qur'an** unless supplied HDF terminology requires another form.
- Introduce HDF as **Human Development Fund (HDF)** on first use when the audience may not know it.
- Explain internal platform names and religious terms on first use.
- Avoid em dashes. Use periods, commas or colons.
- Avoid sentence fragments unless a short closing refrain deliberately benefits from them.
- Do not manufacture transitions to satisfy Yoast.

## Headlines and emphasis

Lead visible titles with human meaning while remaining specific. Do not force the exact focus keyphrase into a title when the SEO title can carry it more naturally.

Bold named tools, programs or platforms on first mention when useful. Bold a closing principle or CTA only when it deserves attention. Do not over-format.

## Calls to action

Use one primary action. It may be donate, view a dashboard, learn more, subscribe, volunteer, or none. Do not assume every post should ask for money.
